Top three activities to do in Dwarka

Explore local markets & Vegas Mall

Sector 12 and Vegas Mall offer a vast range of shopping, street food, and vibrant markets to explore.

Long runs at the District Parks

Large open spaces, like the Sector 9 and Sector 10 parks, are perfect for running, relaxing, and enjoying nature.

DDA Sports Complexes

Access top-tier facilities for cricket, tennis, swimming, and more at the extensive DDA sports centres.
